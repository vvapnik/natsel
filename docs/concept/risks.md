# Risks and open questions

## Known risks

- **Premature convergence.** The population collapses to one lineage. Remedies: speciation as in
  NEAT, niches as in MAP-Elites.
- **Unfair to young species** on slow metrics (retention, revenue).
- **No absolute reference point.** Option: a "fossil" — a frozen species on 1–2% of traffic, for calibration only.
- **Goodhart's law** without a human in the loop: the fitness function and guardrails must be owned by people.
- **Untested combinations** of genes on stage (see [Team workflow](workflow.md)).

## Open questions

- Where to move users of an extinct species (closest living relative?), and the novelty effect after the move.
- Anonymous users and people who log in from several devices.
- Non-stationarity and seasonality when traffic shortage is compensated by longer runs.
